A good move, fast
Each position comes from one of your games: a spot where you thought for a long time or went wrong. You get a few seconds. Any solid move passes; it does not have to be the engine's first choice. Pass three in a row and the timer gets shorter. After you answer, the board unlocks so you can try other moves and step back through the game.
